About This App

Created By Harris, Bunmi and Sallum

Hello, and welcome to TrackMate! Our project involves three models, two YOLOv12n for human and dog breed detection and a face_recognition module that can read 128 unique facial points. The goal of this project is to create a system that can be used to report and identify lost pets and people more rapidly, helping local enforcement agencies. Our next steps will be to receive CASA approval to try and integrate a drone feed element to help locate members who are lost in more isolated areas.
